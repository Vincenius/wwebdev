A Curated List Of Websites For Free SVG Illustrations

December 16, 2019

Updated: June 13, 2024

unDraw Illustrations

An open-source illustrations website, where you can change the colors of the illustrations online before downloading.

visit

ManyPixels

ManyPixels offer free svg illustrations with the possibility to customize the color as well.

illlustrations

100 beautiful illustrations, designed by Vijay Verma during a 100 days of illustrations challenge.

visit

Lukasz Adam

Lukasz Adam is an independent web designer and offers a lot of free svg icon sets on his website.

visit

Blush

A website to create, mix, and customize illustrations made by artists around the world.

visit

Delesign

Royalty-free illustrations, which are customizable by color.

visit

Open Peeps

A hand-drawn illustration library made of building blocks to create your own illustrations.

visit

Pixeltrue

A bunch of cool vector illustrations & animations under the MIT License.

visit

Black Illustrations

A website that focuses on illustrations of black people.

visit

Humaaans

Humaaans have a variety of different svgs of people. They also offer each element of the humans separately to be able to create your own.

visit

Fresh Folk

Similar to Humaaans, Fresh Folk offers people for self composing with a different style.

visit

Open Doodles

Open Doodles provide a bunch of illustrations in a sketchy style.

visit

Getillustrations

Free packs of high-quality, royalty-free vector illustrations.

visit

UI Store Design

43 illustrations resources, which are accumulated from different websites.

visit

IRA Design

A lot of cool illustrations with customizable gradients for the separate parts of the illustration.

visit

Personas

A generator for playful avatars, which is also open source.

visit

Flaticon

A huge database of vector icons and illustrations.

visit

DrawKit

MIT-licensed, hand-drawn vector illustrations for free usage.

visit

Mega Doodles Pack

A collection of over 160 hand-drawn doodle elemens as vector illustrations.

visit

Scribbbles

100+ vectorized scribbbles, which can be added to existing images and illustrations.

visit

Avataaars Generator

Another generator for svg avatars in a different style.

visit

A vector set 30 building illustrations inspired by the scandinavian architecture.
